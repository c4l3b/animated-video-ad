Pulse launch notes
- Uptime + API monitoring for small dev teams. Checks every 30 s from 9 regions.

- Alerts to Slack, PagerDuty, email, SMS. Median time from first failure to alert in our own logs (Oct 2026): 38 s.
- Public status page generated automatically (status.yourcompany.com).
