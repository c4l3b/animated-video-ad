# Shelfie — product facts (from the founder)

- Web + iPhone app for tracking what you read.
- Add a book by scanning its barcode with your phone camera (about 2 seconds).
- Progress bars per book; yearly reading goal; daily reading streaks.
- Stats page: books per month, pages per day, favourite genres.
- Friends: see what friends are reading, share a shelf with one link.
- Beta users (n=1,240, Sept 2026 survey): 68% said they read more since starting Shelfie.
- Free plan; Shelfie Plus is $3/month. Site: shelfie.app
- Never say "the #1 reading app" — we have no data for that.
